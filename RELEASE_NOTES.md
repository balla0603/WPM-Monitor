# WPM Monitor v1.0.0

## 繁體中文

首次公開發行。提供日常打字速度、修正率、估算準確率、速度分布與歷史趨勢統計，支援系統匣背景執行、CSV 匯出及高 DPI 顯示。

**Windows 10 / 11 x64**：解壓縮 ZIP 後執行 EXE。

## English

Initial public distribution. Tracks real-time typing speed, correction rates, estimated accuracy, speed distribution, and historical trends. Includes system tray monitoring, CSV export, and high-DPI support.

**Windows 10 / 11 x64**: Extract the ZIP and launch the executable.
